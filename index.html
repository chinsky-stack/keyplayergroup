How a requirement moves

Four gates. No theater.

01

The requirement is written down: mission, airframe class, data path, and the clause that will kill a non-compliant part.

02

The bill of materials is checked against NDAA country-of-origin limits before a prototype is promised.

03

The package is built for the vehicle that fits: a grant, a pilot, or a direct federal buy. Status of SAM and SDVOSB is stated as it is.

04

Delivery stays inside the record. If a part cannot be traced, it does not ship under our name.
